Rental Protocol & Terms

Standard operating procedures for equipment leasing.

All equipment is inspected prior to dispatch. Clients are responsible for any damages incurred during the rental period beyond normal wear and tear. Repair or replacement costs will be deducted from the security deposit.

A standard 20% security deposit is required for all heavy machinery and premium fragile items. This hold is released within 48 hours of satisfactory equipment return and inspection.

Cancellations made 72 hours prior to dispatch incur no penalty. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a 25% restocking fee. Same-day cancellations will be charged the full logistics rate.
